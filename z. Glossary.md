# PARA

PARA method used here:
- **Projects:** Active, short-term tasks with a clear goal and deadline, such as a sales presentation for a specific date. ([[_Index - Projects]])
- **Areas:** Long-term responsibilities that require ongoing maintenance and have no set end date, such as "Health" or "Finances". ([[_Index - Areas]])
- **Resources:** Topics of interest and reusable information you want to save for future use, like articles on sales tactics or hobbies. ([[_Index - Resources]])
- **Archives:** Inactive items from the other three categories that are no longer needed but may be kept for reference. ([[_Index - Archives]])
