### Resources:

#### Art

- **[Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/):** The "Gold Standard" for free assets. Kenney provides thousands of high-quality, themed packs (pixel art, 3D, UI, and audio) that are all **CC0 (Public Domain)**, meaning you can use them even in commercial games without credit.
- **[Itch.io (Free Assets)](https://itch.io/game-assets/free):** A massive marketplace where artists often post "Free" or "Name your own price" asset packs. It’s excellent for finding unique, niche styles.
- **[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org/):** A community-driven repository for open-source game art. It can be a bit of a "diamond in the rough" experience, but it’s a staple for 2D sprites, tilesets, and icons.
- **[CraftPix.net](https://craftpix.net/freebies/):** Offers a dedicated "Freebies" section with high-quality 2D characters, backgrounds, and UI kits specifically designed for game engines.

#### Sound & Music

- **[Freesound.org](https://freesound.org/):** A massive database of user-uploaded sound effects. **Pro-tip:** Always check the license; some are CC0, but others require **Attribution** (mentioning the creator).
- **[Sonniss (GDC Bundles)](https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc):** Every year during GDC, Sonniss gives away GBs of professional-grade sound effects for free. These are industry-standard and highly recommended.
- **[Bensound](https://www.bensound.com/) or [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/):** Great for background music. Kevin MacLeod (Incompetech) has essentially soundtracked the internet; just remember to follow his attribution rules.
- **[Zapsplat](https://www.zapsplat.com/):** A huge library of over 100,000+ free sound effects and music tracks. It’s very well-organized by category, making it easy to find specific "clinks" or "explosions".
